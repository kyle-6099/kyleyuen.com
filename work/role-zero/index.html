Atomic Role Decomposition for AI-Powered Operations

A methodology for redesigning roles around human-AI collaboration.

Overview

Role Zero is a framework for breaking work down into its smallest useful units — tasks, workflows, and decisions — and reassembling them so humans and AI each handle what they do best. The goal is to redesign roles around collaboration rather than replacement.

The problem with current automation thinking

Most organizations skip the redesign step and go straight to automation. They ask "How do we make this faster?" instead of "Does this process still make sense?" The result is faster versions of broken workflows, not better ones.

The methodology

  • Discovery: Define the role, context, scope, and pain points.
  • Decomposition: Break the role into atomic work units, each small enough that ownership is unambiguous.
  • Mapping: Assign each unit to human, automation, AI agent, or hybrid ownership.
  • Validation: Check coverage, handoffs, and risk.
  • Output: Produce a reusable blueprint and implementation plan.

Current test case: HR Generalist

I am starting with the HR Generalist role: mapping the day-to-day work, separating judgment from repetitive execution, and designing how AI agents handle the routine while the human owns decisions, relationships, and exceptions.

Why this matters

The organizations that get ahead will not be the ones with the most AI tools. They will be the ones that looked honestly at how work is structured and redesigned it for the technology that already exists.
