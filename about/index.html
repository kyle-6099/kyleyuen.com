About

I'm a CPHR candidate and operations leader who has spent over a decade at the intersection of HR, payroll, workforce systems, and office operations. I've been the person called in when a system rollout was going off the rails, when a compliance audit uncovered gaps nobody wanted to talk about, and when a team needed someone who could bridge business needs with what systems can actually deliver.

I have worked across multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction environments covering HR, general office administration, and basic IT support. What sets me apart is that I bring technical literacy to operational problems. I don't just identify inefficiencies; I decompose them into atomic tasks, recommend the right automation or system approach, and work with technical teams to bring it to life. Most operations professionals can tell you what is broken. I can tell you what is broken, why it matters, and what the fix should look like — with the technical literacy to ensure it is actually feasible.
