About

I'm a CPHR candidate and operations leader who's spent over a decade navigating the messy intersection of HR, payroll, workforce systems, and office operations. I've been the person called in when a workforce system rollout was going off the rails, when a multi-jurisdiction compliance audit uncovered gaps nobody wanted to talk about, and when a team needed someone who could bridge the gap between what the business needs and what the systems can actually deliver.

I've worked across multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction environments covering HR, general office administration, and basic IT support. I've led full-cycle payroll implementations, handled employee relations from performance management to terminations, and built compliance frameworks that hold up under audit. I've worked in safety-sensitive industries and cross-border operations where the regulatory landscape shifts at the border.

What sets me apart isn't just the HR knowledge — it's that I bring the technical literacy to evaluate what's possible. I don't just identify inefficiencies; I can decompose them into atomic tasks, recommend the right automation approach, and work with technical teams to bring it to life. Most operations professionals can tell you what's broken. I can tell you what's broken, why it matters, and what the fix should look like — with the technical literacy to ensure it's actually feasible.
