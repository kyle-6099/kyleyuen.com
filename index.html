What I'm good at

🔍

Gap Diagnosis

I look at your entire operation — people, tools, systems, processes — and identify where the real bottlenecks are. Not the symptoms. The root cause. Whether it's a human gap, a tool gap, or a system gap, I find it.

⚙️

Solution Design

I translate business needs into actionable recommendations — whether that's a new process, a workforce system configuration, a compliance framework, or an AI automation opportunity. I bring the technical literacy to evaluate what's feasible, and I work with technical teams to bring it to life.

I decompose roles into atomic tasks, route the automatable ones to AI agents, and build pipelines that handle the routine. Your people focus on judgment and strategy — the system handles everything else.
